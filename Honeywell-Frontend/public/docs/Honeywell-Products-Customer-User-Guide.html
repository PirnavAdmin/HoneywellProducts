Honeywell Products Customer User Guide

Official walkthrough for exploring CCTV and surveillance solutions, customizing security equipment, completing orders, and tracking shipments on Honeywell Products.

Platform: Honeywell Products Audience: Public Customers, Dealers & Integrators
Topic 1

1. Introduction to the Honeywell Products Platform

Honeywell Products provides professional surveillance and security solutions, including Network IP Cameras, Turbo HD Cameras, PTZ Cameras, Solar CCTV kits, DVRs, NVRs, surveillance storage, and networking hardware.

The online storefront enables individual property owners, commercial businesses, system integrators, and institutional partners to browse hardware specifications, verify availability, request quotes, and purchase security hardware online.

Topic 3

3. Searching and Using Smart Filters

  1. Keyword Search: Type product names, SKU codes (e.g. HM-CCTV-PTZ), or specs (4MP, PoE, Solar) in the search bar.
  2. Dynamic Filter Drawer: Filter by Category, Subcategory, Installation (Indoor / Outdoor), Connectivity (PoE, Wi-Fi, 4G), and Features (Night Vision, AI Detection, IP67 Weatherproof).
  3. Sorting: Sort by Featured, Price: Low to High, Price: High to Low, or Customer Rating.
  4. Reset: Click Reset all at any time to restore the entire catalog.
Topic 4

4. Product Details and Availability

Each product detail page (/products/:id) provides:

  • High-Resolution Photo Gallery: Multi-angle view with interactive zoom.
  • Pricing & GST: Clear display of Selling Price (₹), MRP, discount percentage, and GST breakdown.
  • Stock Status: Real-time inventory indicator (In Stock or Out of Stock).
  • Tabbed Specifications: Resolution, sensor type, focal length, IR range, power supply, and enclosure rating.
  • Datasheet Downloads: Direct access to official technical specification sheets (PDF).
Topic 5

5. Managing Your Shopping Cart

Click Add to Cart on any product card or detail page. In your shopping cart (/cart):

  • Use + or - to adjust product quantities in real time.
  • Click the trash icon to remove any unwanted item.
  • Review the subtotal, calculated GST tax, and total payable amount.
  • Cart contents remain preserved in your browser across sessions.
Topic 6

6. Checkout and Placing an Order

  1. Contact Details: Enter your full name, 10-digit mobile number, and email.
  2. Delivery Address: Enter shipping street, city, state, and 6-digit PIN code.
  3. Payment Method: Select your preferred payment option (UPI, Card, Net Banking, QR, Bank Transfer, or COD).
  4. Order Confirmation: Receive an authoritative Order ID (e.g. #ORD-1793014790119) and instant confirmation.
Topic 7

7. Customer Account and Login

Access customer self-service at /account:

  • Registration: Sign up with your Name, Mobile number, Email, and Password.
  • Sign In: Authenticate securely with Email and Password.
  • Address Book: Manage multiple saved delivery addresses.
  • Bank Details: Save refund bank credentials for prompt claim settlements.
Topic 8

8. Payment Methods and Flow

Method How It Works Order Status
UPI (VPA) Enter UPI ID and approve payment on your mobile app. Immediate verification → Processing
Dynamic UPI QR Scan live on-screen QR code with Google Pay, PhonePe, or Paytm. Automatic status check → Processing
Credit & Debit Cards Enter card number, expiry, CVV; simulated 3D Secure OTP verification. Immediate verification → Processing
Net Banking Select from major Indian banks (HDFC, SBI, ICICI, Axis, Kotak). Immediate verification → Processing
Direct Bank Transfer Transfer via NEFT/IMPS to corporate account; submit UTR reference. Placed in Pending Verification until finance team confirms credit.
Cash on Delivery (COD) Pay upon delivery by the carrier. Order booked with payment due on delivery.
Topic 9

9. Order Status, Tracking & Invoices

  • Customer Orders: View past orders, status transitions, and itemized breakdowns at /account/orders.
  • Public Order Tracking: Track any order at /order-tracking using your Order ID and registered mobile/email.
  • Fulfillment Stages: Pending Verification → Processing → Dispatched → In Transit → Delivered.
  • Tax Invoices: Download and print official GST invoices directly from order details.
Topic 10

10. FAQ, Bulk Quotes & Warranty Support

Bulk Quote Requests: For commercial project requirements or quantities exceeding 10 units, use Get a Quote in the header or on product pages.
Warranty Claims: File claims at /warranty or /service-request with your product serial number, invoice reference, and problem description.
